Watch parties

Press play together. Really together.

Everyone plays their own copy of the movie, so it looks exactly as sharp as the file and never buffers over someone’s internet. Infimeety keeps every player on the same moment.

  • Plan it, then start it. Pick the movie and subtitles, everyone gets the file and taps “I’m ready”, and you start it with a 3, 2, 1.
  • Pause lands on the same frame for everyone. So do skips, jumps and starting over.
  • You decide who controls it. Everyone, or just you and your co-hosts. Others can ask: “Play from 1:43:28”, and you tap Accept.
  • Late? Join them. Arrive 30 minutes in and one button puts you exactly where everyone is.
  • Subtitles in SRT, VTT or ASS, big and readable, for everyone at once.

Opening it for the first time
  1. Open the downloaded file and drag Infimeety into Applications.
  2. Open Infimeety from Applications. If macOS says it can’t check the app, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. (On older macOS, Control-click the app and choose Open.)

Questions

Good to know

Is Infimeety free?

Yes. There are no paid plans, no ads and no time limits.

Do my movies and files get uploaded anywhere?

No. They go straight from the sender’s device to yours, encrypted, and are saved in the folder you choose. Our servers only see a file’s name and size, so the chat can show what it is.

Does everyone need the movie for a watch party?

Each person plays their own copy, which is why the picture stays perfect. Getting it takes one tap: copy it from the host right in the lobby. If someone already has the file, they can point Infimeety at it instead.

Which video files play in a watch party?

MP4 (H.264) and WebM play everywhere. MKV files usually play in the Windows app and in Chrome and Edge. On a Mac or in Safari, use MP4.

Can friends on different devices hang out together?

Yes. Windows, Mac, phones and browsers all join the same rooms, calls and watch parties.

Why is a streaming service black when I share my screen?

Services like Netflix protect their videos from screen capture, so they show up black. Watch parties are made for movie files you have.
