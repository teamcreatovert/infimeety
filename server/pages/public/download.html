Opening it on a Mac for the first time
  1. Open the downloaded file and drag Infimeety into Applications.
  2. Open Infimeety from Applications. If macOS says it can’t check the app, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. (On older macOS, Control-click the app and choose Open.)
  3. Allow the microphone and camera when Infimeety asks, so friends can hear and see you.
